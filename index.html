FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before we start building together.

How much does a website cost?

+

Project costs vary based on complexity, features, and timeline. We offer flexible packages starting from budget-friendly options for startups to enterprise solutions. Get in touch for a custom quote tailored to your needs.

How long does development take?

+

Typical projects range from 2–8 weeks depending on scope. Simple sites take 2–3 weeks; complex web applications may require 6–8 weeks or more. We'll give you a clear timeline before we begin.

Do you work with international clients?

+

Absolutely. We proudly serve clients across India, Nepal, USA, and globally. Our team is experienced in remote collaboration across time zones — seamless communication is part of how we work.

What technologies do you use?

+

We use modern, battle-tested technologies including React, Next.js, Node.js, PHP, WordPress, and more — always chosen based on what best fits your specific project requirements and future scale.

Do you provide ongoing support?

+

Yes. We offer comprehensive maintenance plans including security updates, performance optimization, content updates, and 24/7 technical support. We stay with you long after launch.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

+

Guaranteed. Every site we build is fully responsive and optimized across all devices. Mobile-first development is our standard, not an add-on.
